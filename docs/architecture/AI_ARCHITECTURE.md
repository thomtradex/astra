# AI Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the Artificial Intelligence architecture of the Astra Platform.

It describes how intelligence is organized, coordinated and executed.

Artificial Intelligence is a platform capability.

Not a product.

Not a feature.

It exists to amplify organizational intelligence.

---

# Mission

Build an Artificial Intelligence platform capable of understanding organizations.

Learning continuously.

Reasoning reliably.

Planning intelligently.

Executing safely.

Remaining independent from any specific AI provider.

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence is not the center of Astra.

The Company Brain is.

Artificial Intelligence consumes knowledge.

It never owns knowledge.

Artificial Intelligence executes reasoning.

It never replaces business.

Organizations remain in control.

Always.

---

# AI Principles

Every AI capability follows these principles.

Context First.

Knowledge First.

Memory First.

Reasoning Before Action.

Planning Before Execution.

Governance Always.

Explainability Always.

Model Independence.

Human Oversight.

Continuous Learning.

---
